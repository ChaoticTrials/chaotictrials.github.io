## Overview
SimplyHarvesting is a lightweight and simple Minecraft mod designed to improve farming efficiency. With this mod
installed, you can harvest fully grown crops and replant them automatically with just a right-click. It eliminates the
hassle of manually breaking and replanting crops, allowing players to farm with greater convenience.

---

## Features
**Right-Click Harvesting**
- Fully grown crops can be harvested with a simple right-click. The mod automatically identifies when crops are ready to
be harvested and replants new seeds.

**Automatic Replanting**
- After harvesting any fully mature crop, the mod immediately replants it without requiring the player to carry seeds.

## Known Limitations
The mod only supports vanilla crops (other modded crops may not be recognized unless they use the vanilla crop
mechanics). If requested, additional crop support may require compatibility updates.

## Ignore Harvesting
There's the block tag `#simplyharvesting:ignore_harvesting` that can be used to ignore harvesting on certain blocks. By
default, it's filled with the block tag `#c:berry_bushes`. That's filled with some berry bushes I found in other mods.
If you encounter issues with other bushes, report them on GitHub; I'll add them to the ignore list. Until they are
added, you can toggle the `disableOnSneaking` option in the server config to disable harvesting while sneaking.
