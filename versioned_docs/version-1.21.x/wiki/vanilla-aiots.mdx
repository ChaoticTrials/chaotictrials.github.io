## Special Features
AIOTs are all-in-one tools that combine the functionality of a Sword, Shovel, Pickaxe, Axe, and Hoe into a single item.
With an AIOT, you can harvest almost anything effortlessly. By default, their durability is five times that of the base
material, but this can be customized in the configuration settings.

## Compatibility
### Simplest Copper Gear
If the Simplest Copper Gear mod is installed, a Copper AIOT will be included.

### [MoreVanillaTools](morevanillatools.mdx)
AIOTs are available for every material introduced by the MoreVanillaTools mod, including all the special features unique
to each material.
