This mod introduces a variety of new mechanics, enchantments, and blocks to enhance your Minecraft experience. Dive
into a world of curses, energies, and lightning-powered effects.

## Features
### Maledictus Aufero
On lightning strike the Maledictus Aufero scans enchantable items within three blocks, removes random curse enchantments
from cursed items, and transfers those curses to compatible nearby items (pairwise if `onlyTransferCurses` is `true`),
summoning visual-only colored lightning on affected items.
After transfers, it halves the stored [negative energy](#negative-energy) on items with the EnergyCollector component
(if enabled), spawning green visual lightning on items that didn’t receive normal transfer lightning.

### Enchantments
The mod adds several new enchantments, each imbued with unique curses that provide intriguing gameplay challenges:

**Curse of Karma**
- The attacker will take the same amount of damage dealt to the victim. This curse serves as a form of karmic retribution
and requires careful consideration during battles.

**Curse of Kindness**
- Instead of inflicting damage, the attacker will end up healing the target. This benevolent curse twists violence into
acts of unintended kindness.

**Curse of Randomness**
- Whenever the item loses durability, its enchantments are shuffled unpredictably, though curses remain unaffected. This
curse introduces a chaotic and thrilling element to enchanted gear.

### Negative Energy
:::info
**Negative Energy** can be disabled in the config file
:::

A hidden attribute called Negative Energy affects items, making them susceptible to corruption and curses over time.
This energy builds up under specific conditions, including:

- Being in the Nether, where merely holding items adds to their negative energy.
- Hurting undead mobs, which gradually transfers negative energy to the attacking item.

To combat the effects of Negative Energy, the Maledictus Aufero can purify items and reduce their accumulated energy.
Effective management of this energy is crucial to prevent items from succumbing to curses.

## Configurations
You can adjust various settings in the mod via its configuration file. Below is the configuration table:

| **Key**                         | **Default Value** | **Description**                                                              |
|---------------------------------|-------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| `safeLightnings`                | `true`            | Ensures lightning is purely visual, preventing item burning during a strike. |
| `karmaChance`                   | `0.2` (20%)       | Probability of the Curse of Karma activating.                                |
| `kindnessChance`                | `0.4` (40%)       | Probability of the Curse of Kindness activating.                             |
| `minCurseDelay`                 | `6000`            | Minimum time (ticks) before an item can be cursed again.                     |
| `maxCurseDelay`                 | `12000`           | Maximum time (ticks) before an item can be cursed again.                     |
| `onlyTransferCurses`            | `false`           | Ensures curses are only transferred, not just removed from the item.         |
| `NegativeEnergy.enabled`        | `true`            | Enables or disables the Negative Energy system.                              |
| `NegativeEnergy.netherAddition` | `0.00001`         | Rate of negative energy accumulation in the Nether (per second).             |
| `NegativeEnergy.hurtAddition`   | `0.0001`          | Negative energy added when hurting undead mobs (per hit).                    |
