## Changelog
UtilitiX 1.21.1 is out! Grab it like it's hot!

### These features were removed from 1.20 to 1.21
- No more shifting with a shovel to prevent flattening blocks
- No more illusioners in raids
- The buggy backpack is gone

### These new features were added
- A complete feature config where you can enable/disable every feature
- The Quiver finally arrived
- The glued blocks limit is now configurable
- You can drain even more xp out of your player by requiring levels for the Mob Yoinker instead of just points
- The Crude Furnace recipes are now in JEI
- Linked Crystals now stack up to 16
- Printing Maps scale is now a client config and can be overridden in the command itself
- Two new buttons in experience crystal
  - Repair Mending item in the main hand 
  - Repair all Mending items in your inventory
